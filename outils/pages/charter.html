Session charter

This charter protects everyone: you, the other participants and your teacher. It applies to all SAFE sessions, at your home or online. Each participant accepts it when booking, including in a group.

Before the session

  • Tell us anything that may matter for your practice: injury, pain, pregnancy, recent surgery, current treatment. You can do so in the booking form (optional field) or at the start of the session.
  • Avoid a heavy meal in the two hours before.
  • Wear comfortable, appropriate clothing. Practice is barefoot or in socks.

Practice space, at your home

  • A clear space of about 2 m by 1 m per person, on a clean, non-slip floor.
  • A room at a comfortable temperature and, if possible, quiet.
  • Pets are kept away during the session.
  • Only registered people take part. Anyone unexpected may only join with the teacher's agreement.
  • If you have no mat, we bring one.

Practice space, online

  • A clear space, a stable connection, the camera placed so the teacher can see your movements.
  • You are responsible for the safety of your space (floor, obstacles, furniture).
  • Online sessions are not recorded.

Mutual respect

  • Everyone behaves respectfully, in words and gestures.
  • Any inappropriate remark or gesture, whether sexual, insulting, discriminatory or threatening, ends the session immediately. The session then remains due.
  • The teacher may refuse to start or may stop a session if safety conditions are not met: a participant under the influence of alcohol or a substance, an aggressive animal, threatening behaviour, unexpected people, a dangerous place. The session then remains due.
  • You may stop the session at any time.

Physical contact

  • No physical contact without your agreement, asked for verbally during the session.
  • You may withdraw this agreement at any time without giving a reason. Adjustments are then given verbally.

Listen to your body

  • You practise at your own pace and within your limits. No posture is compulsory.
  • Report any pain, dizziness or discomfort straight away, and stop.

Minors

No minimum age. A minor only takes part with the written agreement of a parent or legal guardian, who must be present.

Photos, videos and recordings

No photos, videos or recordings during the session without the agreement of everyone present, including the teacher.

Confidentiality

What is said during the session stays between us.

What our sessions are not

Our sessions do not replace medical or psychological care. We do not diagnose or prescribe any treatment. Emergency (France): 15. Suicidal thoughts: 3114.

Health statement

Each participant confirms it when booking:

I declare that:

  • I know that yoga works the body and may cause stiffness, pain or, more rarely, an injury, especially if I go beyond my limits;
  • I am fit for gentle physical activity, or I have asked my doctor if in doubt, in particular in case of pregnancy, recent surgery, heart or blood pressure problems, back or joint problems, dizziness or epilepsy;
  • I will tell SAFE before the session about any change in my health;
  • I will practise at my own pace, respect my limits and report any discomfort during the session;
  • I know that SAFE sessions do not replace medical or psychological care.
