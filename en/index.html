Our approach

SAFE brings together body, mind and the human side. We start from you: your body, your breath, your pace. Each session begins with a short conversation, then the practice adapts to how you feel that day.

No performance, no judgement: a moment to breathe, find your balance and move forward.

Our sessions do not replace medical or psychological care. Emergency (France): 15. Suicidal thoughts: 3114.

Your questions

Do I need a yoga mat?

No. If you do not have one, we bring it free of charge, up to 6 mats.

Do I need any experience?

No. The session adapts to your body and experience, whether you are a beginner or not. Tell us about any injury, pain or pregnancy when booking or at the start of the session.

How much is travel?

Free anywhere in Marseille. Beyond, up to Aix-en-Provence and Aubagne, it costs €0.30 per round-trip kilometre, once per session, calculated automatically when you enter your address. Further away: contact us for a quote.

Can we book as a group?

Yes, up to 6 people at one of your homes. The price per person drops from 2 people. Everyone can pay their own share, online or in cash.

How does an online session work?

On video, with a link sent by email. Clear some space and place your camera so we can see your movements. Sessions are not recorded.

How do I pay?

Online by PayPal or bank card (no PayPal account needed), or in cash on the day.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before the session, with the link in your email. After that, the session is due.
